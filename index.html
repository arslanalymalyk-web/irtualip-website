Questions buyers ask first.

What should I look for in a technology consultancy for custom software?

Look for a team that starts from your business goal, shows working software early, is open about trade-offs and is still answering the phone after launch.

Ask who will do the work, how they handle security, and what you own at the end, including code and data.

How can AI make enterprise software more efficient?

By taking on repetitive work, such as classifying documents, routing requests and flagging anomalies, and by giving people better information at the moment they decide.

The gains are largest when AI connects to your existing systems, data and workflows.

What is cloud migration?

Moving applications, data and workloads from on-premises servers, or from one cloud to another, into a cloud environment. It ranges from a straight move to redesigning an application so it uses cloud-native services.

Why should I consider moving to the cloud?

Common reasons are scalability, resilience, faster delivery and paying for what you use. It also makes AI services easier to adopt.

Not every workload belongs in the cloud, so we assess each one on its merits.

How long does cloud migration take?

It depends on how many applications you have, how tangled their dependencies are and how much you want to change along the way. We size it after an assessment and give a number only then.

How does cloud migration affect data security?

Done well, it improves it. Encryption, identity controls, logging and automated compliance checks are easier to apply consistently.

The risk sits in the move itself, so we plan access, data handling and rollback before anything shifts.

How do you protect our data when building AI?

Security is part of design, build and deployment. We work under ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II controls, and we agree how your data is accessed, stored and used before work begins.

What happens after launch?

We can provide managed support and monitoring, or hand the system over to your team with documentation and training. We agree which at the start, so there are no surprises.
