Why AI pilots stall, and how to avoid it

Virtual IP team4 min readSample content

A pilot that impresses in a demo can still fail in the real world. The causes are rarely about the model.

Five common causes

  • No owner. Name a business owner before the first sprint.
  • No baseline. Measure today's process so results mean something.
  • Prototype data. Test on data that looks like production, not a clean sample.
  • Late security review. Involve security and compliance at the start.
  • No plan to run it. Decide who monitors, supports and improves the system after launch.

Every one of these is cheaper to fix in week one than in month six.
